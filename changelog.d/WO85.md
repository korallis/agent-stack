### Fixed
- `agent-proxy-status` and `rig-console` read Codex's quota windows by their reported length, not their position.
  - Codex now sends its weekly window as "primary" (10080 minutes) and a 0-minute "secondary": it has no 5-hour
    limit. Every Codex account showed 5h = 100% (red in the console) and weekly = 0%; it now shows no 5-hour reading
    and the real weekly figure. The older shape (no window lengths) is read as before.
  - An account that has used up a window but has credits is shown as on credits (`in use` in the table, "on credits"
    in the console's info colour), never red or "over", and stays eligible. A used-up Codex window with credits
    reported as none is over its limit.
  - `--json` adds `short_window_minutes`, `weekly_window_minutes`, `has_credits`, `credits_unlimited` and
    `on_credits`; the table gains a credits column.
  - The console's Pool and Home account panels show how many accounts run on credits (a cost signal), and the Pool
    lists them.
