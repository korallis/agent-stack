### Changed
- The Playwright MCP writes to one dir per seat, `~/.local/state/agent-stack/playwright-mcp/<seat>/` (0700), instead of
  one dir shared by every seat and project. Both runtimes start it through `agent-playwright-mcp`, which reads the seat
  from `OPENRIG_SESSION_NAME` (from an ancestor process when the runtime trims the MCP's environment) and replaces
  `--output-dir`. A seat's network/console scratch dir is now its `net/` subdir; the credential guard protects every
  `net/` tree there, including the old one and what `--migrate` moved.
- New: `agent-playwright-retention` and an hourly timer delete MCP files after 48 h, and in client-data rigs' dirs after
  6 h (`~/.config/agent-stack/playwright-retention.env`: `KEEP_HOURS`, `CLIENT_DATA_HOURS`, `CLIENT_DATA_RIGS`).
  `install.sh --apply` moves (never deletes) the shared dir's files untouched for an hour into `unattributed-<date>/`.
  QA and witness guidance: copy evidence you keep into the slice's proof dir.
- Seats pick the new MCP command up when they relaunch; nothing breaks before that. Their old MCP's network/console dir
  (`playwright-mcp/net/<seat>/`) stays allowed and in place, and retention ages it with the rest.
