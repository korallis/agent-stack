### Added
- Proof attachments are documented and taught (WO69). An owner update row can carry one screenshot, video or PDF
  (`--evidence-ref <absolute path>`, at most 50 MiB) into its Slack thread. CULTURE, the QA and lead guidance, the
  witness slice and the agent-stack skill say when to send one (a witness pass, a finished user-visible feature, a fix
  for a bug the owner reported; not every step), how (Playwright screenshot and video recipes), where to save it (the
  seat's `proof/` dir under `$HOME/.cache/<rig>-tmp/<seat>/`, not a job's temp dir, which its trap removes before the
  daemon posts; and kept after the row posts, since "posted" confirms the text before the file is read: proof files
  age out after 7 days) and what never to show (secrets, tokens, real client data; demo or fictional data only).
- Slack setup covers attachments both ways: the app needs `files:write` (proof seats send) and `files:read` (files the
  owner sends); `rig slack setup --required-scopes chat:write,channels:history,channels:read,files:read,files:write` makes
  `rig slack verify` check them (its default list does not); then `openrig-slack-upload-check` and `--live`. In the
  README, REFERENCE and the onboarding skill (new step 3).
