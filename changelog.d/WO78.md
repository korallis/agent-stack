### Added
- `rig-console`, a read-only fleet console (phase 1 of WO78), installed as a launcher on the pinned Node:
  - **Mission Control.** Hero tiles for working, idle, stuck, blocked, owner decisions and gate decisions today.
    One card per rig shows seat dots per pod and its health. The screen also has work in flight by role, owner
    decisions, the account pool, system health, the event log and a live ticker.
  - **Seat Matrix.** Every seat of every rig, with context use shaded (red at 80%+), activity glyphs, 24 h braille
    telemetry from a small local ring buffer, the subscription pool, context pressure, the queue by rig and an
    event tape.
  - **Data.** One cache reads the daemon API (nodes without the per-seat capture options), its live events (joined
    near the tail), the Jev decision log, `agent-proxy-status` and `agent-heavy status`. It refreshes every 5 s,
    never under 2 s, and backs off when the daemon is slow. It never polls tmux.
  - **Rendering.** Diffed redraws in the alternate screen, truecolor with 256/16-colour and NO_COLOR fallbacks.
    The layout is graceful at 120 columns and best at 176+.
  - **Demo.** `console/fixtures/demo.json` is a neutral demo fleet; `--once --fixture` draws it.
