### Fixed
- `openrig-upgrade 0.6.3 --no-restart` restarted the daemon (WO68): option parsing stopped at the version, so a flag
  after it was ignored. Options now count before or after the version, and parsing is strict like `./install.sh`: an
  unknown option, a second version or an empty argument exits 2 before anything runs, and `-h`/`--help` prints the
  usage and does nothing.
