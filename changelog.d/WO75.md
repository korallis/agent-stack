### Changed
- The Jev merge gate knows test-first work (WO75, owner decision 2026-10-01). `review.merge_gate` v2: a tests-only PR
  (tests, journeys, their fixtures and helpers; no application, schema, build or production file) may merge when
  review, QA and CI are green on its head and QA says its tests fail only at the step that needs the not-yet-built
  feature. It still holds when non-test code changes, a test fails at a step that is already built, the evidence names
  no failing step (a bare "2 failed" names none), or any required CI check fails. Measured with live diagnosis calls
  (test/fixtures/merge-gate-tests-first/RESULTS.md): the reported PR moves from hold to merge, every look-alike stays
  hold. Applies to every project.
