### Changed
- The Jev merge gate knows test-first work (WO75, owner decision 2026-10-01). `review.merge_gate` v2: a tests-only PR may merge
  when review, QA and CI are green on its head and QA actually ran its tests and saw them fail only at the step that needs the
  not-yet-built feature. Tests-only is a fact, not a reading: `agent-merge-evidence` adds `scope (from the diff): tests-only` (or
  `NOT tests-only` with the paths) to the gate's input. It still holds when non-test code changes, a test fails at an
  already-built step, the evidence names no failing step, QA only predicted the failure, QA's verdict is FAIL or missing, or a
  required check fails. Measured with live diagnosis calls (test/fixtures/merge-gate-tests-first/RESULTS.md).
- `agent-merge-evidence` checks the deterministic gates (required checks, review verdict, QA PASS for the head, mergeable) in
  the act band too; before, a live Jev merge in the act band passed without them.
