### Fixed
- `agent-project-check` no longer reports parked-row wakes as duplicate merge sweeps or daily summaries (WO71). It
  counted every periodic-reminder at 900 s / 86400 s, and `rig queue block --wake-after 15m|24h` registers those
  too; when a job's spec couldn't be read (e.g. a slow daemon), the wake was counted. Watchdogs are now identified by
  their spec message (`Merge sweep:` / `Daily summary:`), whatever the interval; a job whose spec can't be read is
  named as not counted, never guessed; a sweep on the wrong seat or a second one is reported with its seats.
