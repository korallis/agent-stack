### Fixed
- `agent-proxy-status` reads quota headers by provider, never by magnitude. Anthropic's are fractions (0.29; 1.01 when
  over the limit) and Codex's are percents ("100"). It printed Anthropic's 1.01 as "1%" while the account was over its
  limit. Past 100% the table now says `OVER`, and such an account isn't counted in the `eligible:` line, which notes
  "(n over limit)". `--json` keeps the raw header values and adds `short_window_pct`, `weekly_pct` and `over_limit`; a
  reading that is not a finite number (NaN, Infinity, an overflow) is `null`, so the JSON stays valid.
- The dispatcher (`eligibleFamilies`) and `agent-recover`'s pool check no longer count an account past its limit
  (`over_limit`) as eligible; one shared rule (`accountEligible`). An older status tool without the field still counts.
