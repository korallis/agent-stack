### Fixed
- The OpenRig daemon no longer spends a third of its main thread forking tmux for the structural activity sweep
  (WO74). The 5-second sweep ran one `tmux capture-pane` per running seat (plus a second, ANSI capture for each seat
  it couldn't classify); on a 73-seat host that stalled `/healthz` and `rig send`. New patch 142 captures all seats
  with one listing and one chained tmux call per 24 seats, then one chained ANSI call for the unclassified ones:
  about 3 forks per sweep. A missing or failing session is handled exactly as before, and only the seats of a
  failed chunk fall back to per-seat capture.
