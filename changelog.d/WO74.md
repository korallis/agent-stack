### Fixed
- The OpenRig daemon no longer spends a third of its main thread forking tmux for the structural activity sweep
  (WO74). The 5-second sweep ran one `tmux capture-pane` per running seat (plus a second, ANSI capture for each seat
  it couldn't classify); on a 73-seat host that stalled `/healthz` and `rig send`. New patch 142 lists the sessions
  once and captures the live seats in chained tmux calls of at most 24 (then the unclassified ones the same way):
  1 + ceil(live/24) + ceil(unknown/24) calls per sweep, e.g. 4 instead of 72 for 72 seats' plain pass. A chunk that
  fails (output over the 1 MiB exec buffer, or a session gone mid-sweep) is split in half and retried; a missing or
  failing session is handled exactly as before.
